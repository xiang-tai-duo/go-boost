Font Awesome Free (webfonts)
============================
The following files are used by electron/wwwroot but are NOT committed to this
MIT-licensed repository because they are distributed under their own licenses
(SIL OFL 1.1 for font files, MIT for CSS/code, CC BY 4.0 for icons):

  fa-brands-400.ttf
  fa-brands-400.woff2
  fa-regular-400.ttf
  fa-regular-400.woff2
  fa-solid-900.ttf
  fa-solid-900.woff2
  font-awesome.min.css

All of these are listed in .gitignore.

How to obtain:
  Project home : https://fontawesome.com/
  Source / releases (GitHub):
    https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/releases
  Free download (official):
    https://use.fontawesome.com/releases/v6.7.2/fontawesome-free-6.7.2-web.zip

After downloading fontawesome-free-<version>-web.zip:
  1. Extract the archive.
  2. Copy webfonts/fa-*.ttf, webfonts/fa-*.woff2 and css/all.min.css
     into this directory (electron/wwwroot/webfonts/).
  3. Rename css/all.min.css to font-awesome.min.css
     (the HTML references ./webfonts/font-awesome.min.css).
